# Sample document: Refund policy (fictional)

This is sample data for the demo. It is fictional and is not a real company policy.

## Refunds

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Exchanges

Items can be exchanged within 30 days if they are unused and in their original packaging. Digital downloads cannot be exchanged once they have been downloaded.

## How to request a refund

Send an email to support@example.com with your order number and the reason for the refund. The support team replies within 2 business days.
